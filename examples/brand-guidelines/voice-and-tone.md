# IBM Brand Voice & Tone — Excerpt

## Voice Pillars

**Confident, not arrogant.** We've been doing this for 100+ years. We don't need to prove it in every sentence.

**Plain-spoken.** Use everyday words. If a sentence needs decoding, rewrite it.

**Specific, not aspirational.** "We help banks reduce fraud detection time from days to minutes" beats "We empower transformative financial outcomes."

**Curious.** Ask questions a smart customer would ask. Then answer them concretely.

## Don't

- Adjective stacking ("transformative, scalable, intelligent...")
- Buzzwords without context (synergy, holistic, paradigm)
- Vague benefit claims ("unleash potential")
- Em-dash mid-sentence as a crutch — use a period if you can

## Do

- Lead with the customer's problem, not the product
- Use numbers when you have them
- Use short sentences when the idea is heavy
- Use one CTA per ad, not three

## Tagline patterns that work

- "[Specific outcome]. With [product]."
- "Built for [audience that's underserved]."
- "[Customer name] [verb] [number] with [product]."

## Tagline patterns to avoid

- "Transforming [industry]"
- "The future of [anything]"
- "Reimagining [process]"
